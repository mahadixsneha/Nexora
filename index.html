Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Nexora Android app ("Nexora", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Nexora is developed by MrDevilEx (independent developer, Bangladesh). By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Account details: email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text), username, display name, profile photo, bioTo create and secure your account and show your profile to other people
Content you create: posts, comments, photos, stories, reels, sounds/music clips, voice messages, text messages and reactionsTo provide the social, chat, story and reel features and show them to the people you choose
Friends, groups and activity: friend requests, group membership, likes, notifications, read receipts ("seen"), typing status, online status (can be turned off in settings)To make chats, groups and notifications work
Calls: call and group-call signalling data (who is calling whom, call time and type). Audio and video are sent directly between devices (peer-to-peer) and may pass through relay (TURN) servers; we do not record calls.To connect audio and video calls
Device and app data: push notification token, app version numberTo send you notifications and to deliver app updates
Reports and moderation: reports you send about content or users, and actions taken by moderatorsTo keep the community safe

We do not collect your precise location, contacts list, or financial information. We do not show third-party advertising.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: for video/audio calls, recording voice messages and reels, and taking photos. Only used when you start those features.
  • Photos / storage: to pick and upload images and videos you choose to share, and to save media.
  • Notifications: to alert you about messages, calls, likes, comments and announcements.
  • Bluetooth: to route call audio to your Bluetooth headset.
  • Install unknown apps: used only for the in-app update feature, so Nexora can download a new version of itself and open the Android installer. You always confirm the installation yourself.
  • Foreground service, full-screen intent, battery optimisation: to keep calls running and show incoming calls reliably.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve Nexora.
  • To deliver messages, calls, notifications and announcements from the Nexora team.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce our rules, and handle reports, suspensions and bans.
  • To deliver app updates.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your profile, posts and reels are visible to other users according to the app's features and your settings.
  • Chats are visible only to the people in that conversation. Members of a group can see group messages and who has read them.
  • Nexora moderators can review reported content and may access content needed to handle a report.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users' safety.

5. Service providers

Nexora uses trusted third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase: authentication, database and file storage.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
  • WebRTC relay (TURN) servers: helping calls connect when a direct connection is not possible.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention

  • Stories disappear after 24 hours, and reels are automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Other content stays until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Call signalling data is temporary and removed after the call ends.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, delete your posts, reels, stories and messages, block users, and turn notification categories and online status on or off in the app.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account inside the app (Settings). This removes your profile and the content linked to it.
  • You can also contact us (below) to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access rules on our database so that people can only see what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Nexora is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
